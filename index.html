Spectrogram: brighter means louder. Newest sound is at the top.

Left to right is pitch

Low rumbles sit on the left, hiss and sibilance on the right. The scale is logarithmic, so every octave gets the same width.

Height is loudness

A taller bar means more energy at that pitch, measured in decibels relative to full scale. Silence sits at the bottom.

Try a few sounds

Whistle for one sharp peak, hum for a stack of evenly spaced harmonics, clap for a burst across the whole range.
